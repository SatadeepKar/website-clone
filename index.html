FAQ

Common questions

How is Frugal Testing different from other QA vendors?

We staff engagements with senior testers only, scope work around your actual release risk instead of a fixed package, and get a test plan running within days — not the multi-week onboarding typical of larger QA vendors.

Do you work with early-stage startups or only enterprises?

Both. Engagement models range from a single sprint of manual testing for an early-stage product to a dedicated, always-on QA pod for enterprise release cycles.

What engagement models do you offer?

Hourly, fixed-scope per release, or a dedicated monthly QA pod embedded with your engineering team — whichever matches how your roadmap moves.

How quickly can testing start?

Most engagements move from the initial consultation to a scoped test plan within 48 hours, with testing beginning the same week.
