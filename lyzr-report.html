How Lyzr is different

1. Governance built in

LangChain, CrewAI and AutoGen require teams to build audit trails, cost controls and access policy themselves. Lyzr ships them (Control Plane, RAI).

2. Deploy anywhere, incl. air-gapped

On-prem/VPC and the Optimus appliance suit regulated buyers. Workato is hosted-only per the reference doc.

3. Agent-native, not bolted on

Agents are the core abstraction, unlike integration-first iPaaS tools that added AI later (Workato's Genies).

4. Framework-agnostic control

The Control Plane can govern agents built elsewhere, so Lyzr can sit above existing investments.

5. Low-code to speed adoption

Business-friendly studio and 100–200+ blueprints versus code-first frameworks.

6. Low entry cost

Free and $19/$99 tiers allow trial before enterprise commitment, unlike demo-gated pricing from Workato and UnifyApps.

Trade-offs. Developer-heavy teams may prefer the flexibility of LangChain/CrewAI. Lyzr's connector breadth (around 200 native tools) is far below Workato (14,000+ apps) or UnifyApps (950+ connectors). Many figures are vendor-reported.
